## Rules for later phases

1. New content goes under `Expansion` (in `scenes/level/expanded_library_expansion.tscn`), placed in level coordinates.
2. It uses the level's existing managers through their groups (`ObjectiveManager.find()`, `CheckpointManager.find()`, `NoiseSystem.find()`, …), never a second copy.
3. A stateful mechanic joins the `checkpoint_state` group with `get_checkpoint_state()` and `restore_checkpoint_state()`.
4. Interactables must exist when the level loads: the HUD and AudioDirector connect to them once, deferred after load.
5. Solid geometry that guards must walk around requires a navmesh rebake. That changes the baseline's navmesh, so it needs approval.
6. Shared scripts keep their defaults, so the tutorial behaves the same.
7. A change to anything outside `Expansion` needs approval, new baseline digests in `tests/expanded_baseline_tests.gd`, and a note here.
