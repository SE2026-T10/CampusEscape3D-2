# Prompts ảnh concept bảo tàng — v1

Tạo bằng công cụ imagegen tích hợp. Ảnh dùng để hình dung không gian; mặt bằng SVG/PNG là tài liệu tham chiếu cho kích thước và đường đi. Hai ảnh là diễn giải cùng một thiết kế hư cấu, không phải ảnh chụp Godot hay mô hình 3D có thể nhập vào game.

## 01 — Phối cảnh tách tầng

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: high quality 3D architectural game level concept, exploded axonometric overview of a fictional museum for a Godot stealth game. One complete landscape image, approximately 3:2.
Primary request: render a BEAUTIFULLY LEGIBLE TWO-LEVEL EXPLODED ARCHITECTURAL DIORAMA, showing both floor plates fully, upper level floated above ground level in alignment so the viewer can understand the entire museum layout. Exactly two playable floors. Inspired by an Italian Renaissance courtyard museum in Boston, circa 1990, a fictional game layout, not an exact reconstruction of the Gardner museum.
Style: polished stylized low-poly 3D game environment, modular architecture, simplified clean geometry, warm ochre plaster, muted burgundy and forest-green gallery walls, pale stone columns, dark wooden floorboards, brass picture frames, believable human-scale props. Attractive restrained cinematic lighting, clear geometry and cut edges. Dark desaturated navy studio background.
Geometric invariants: rectangular building footprint 36m east-west by 30m north-south. Center is a 12m x12m open-air courtyard, with plants and simple stone paving ONLY on ground level. Upper floor has a real open void directly above it, never a second garden floor. A 3m wide square circulation arcade wraps the courtyard on each floor. Two aligned stair cores: A southwest, B northeast. Normal floor heights 0m and 4.5m; exploded spacing is diagrammatic. Show both floor plates with exposed interior walls only about waist-high so routes/door openings are readable; tall columns may remain. Remove all roofs.
Orientation: view from southeast, north toward upper right/back, east at right foreground. Put a small compass N/E outside the model if needed to keep orientation unambiguous.
Ground floor room layout clockwise from north: a long history gallery across the north wing, northeast stair B, east staff room with desk and a tiny golden key marker K, small southeast service rooms, south central entrance lobby, southwest stair A, long west sculpture gallery. Ground floor entrance at middle of south exterior wall. Courtyard accessed from south arcade.
Upper floor same outer footprint: north master painting gallery with T3, northeast stair B connecting through north gallery, east painting gallery with T2, southeast restoration workshop with rope coil marker R, south upper gallery, southwest stair A connecting through south gallery, west painting gallery with T1. Put all three framed artwork target markers T1 T2 T3 in their specified gallery. These are three separate rooms. Doorways visibly connect rooms to ring arcade; west and north galleries have two access doorways each. East gallery has one keyed arcade entry and a door into workshop. Workshop additionally reachable from south upper gallery.
Escape: ONLY southeast workshop's EAST EXTERIOR window, facing outside the building, has a rope down to an external small stone landing at the east side. It must NOT descend into the courtyard. Show a clear exterior landing beyond east wall on ground plate. Keep rope diagram readable across exploded separation, subtle dashed connection permitted.
Gameplay props: large opaque stone plinths and transparent display cases with solid wood bases, spaced so a person can walk around, plus columns and framed paintings. A few tiny faceless guard silhouettes for scale. Ground gallery holds sculpture; upper galleries hold paintings; workshop holds easel/crates/rope. No combat, no weapons, no futuristic equipment.
Composition: upper plate above and slightly behind ground plate, ample breathing room, both fully visible, no overlapping floor interiors. A few thin vertical alignment guides connecting the matching stair cores. The upper plate's center is an obvious hollow square, revealing the absence of a floor. Main priority is spatial clarity and game production usefulness.
Text: only small neat labels "01 / GROUND", "02 / UPPER", "A", "B", "T1", "T2", "T3", "K", "R", "EXIT". No other paragraphs or dense text. This is an architectural 3D concept render, NOT a flat floorplan or blueprint. Avoid extra storeys, impossible stair connections, narrow unusable corridors, glass treated as opaque cover, heavy shadows hiding rooms, watermark.
```

## 02 — Góc nhìn người chơi

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: immersive 3D environment concept art for a first-person Godot stealth game, landscape approximately 16:9.
Primary request: a highly readable player-eye-level view from the FIRST UPPER FLOOR south arcade of the SAME fictional two-storey Italian Renaissance courtyard museum at night, looking diagonally northwest across a central courtyard void. Communicate how hiding, observing a patrol and moving between art rooms will feel.
Architecture invariant: exactly ground floor plus one upper floor, upper floor at 4.5m above courtyard ground. Building footprint 36m by30m, central open-air courtyard 12mx12m, covered circulation arcade 3m wide on each level with pale stone columns and waist-high balustrades on upper level. Courtyard ground below holds plants, paving, one modest stone fountain, moonlight. Across the courtyard show ONE opposing upper gallery/balcony and the ground arcade below it. Open sky visible above, no third tier, no roofs blocking view into courtyard.
View composition: camera 1.6m above upper south corridor floor. Foreground left reveals an open doorway into west painting gallery: burgundy plaster, gilded frames, a museum display case with a tall OPAQUE dark wood base and transparent glass above; the base plausibly hides a crouched person, glass remains visibly see-through. Large gallery interior continues behind doorway, readable 1.5m-plus passage around display. Foreground right is courtyard balustrade with a tall stone column breaking sightline. Leading lines of parquet floor and repeating arches guide the eye around the ring.
One unarmed uniformed museum security guard, small at middle distance on west arcade, walking away from camera at an oblique angle; visible and readable human-scale figure, no attacking, no combat. A target painting subtly highlighted by its warm picture lamp visible in west gallery, no glowing fantasy effect. No player hands or weapons. Tiny period appropriate wall sconces and framed works, no modern digital screens or lasers.
Art direction: polished stylized low-poly 3D game screenshot / environment art, simplified modular geometry easy to build in Godot, restrained texture detail, warm ivory stone, dark walnut parquet, burgundy walls and antique brass. Soft amber lamps contrast with cool blue moonlight entering the courtyard. Enough ambient light that navigation, doorway, guard and cover all remain clearly readable. Quiet suspense, elegant rather than horror. No text, no HUD, no labels, no watermark. No real museum claims.
Important: actual standing-height architectural perspective, not isometric, not overhead, not a real photo. Wide lens about 28mm but no fisheye. Make the view consistent with a TWO-level museum and the described courtyard ring design.
```
